# Physical cable suspension and load attachment

Reviewed: 2026-09-24, rigid-body pivot revision following the playable handling review.

`scripts/crane/cable_body_2d.gd` is a caller-shaped `RigidBody2D` with a force-integration callback. `suspension_2d.gd` applies a massless, tension-only cable constraint at that body's local pivot. Godot owns gravity, inertia, collision response and ordinary translation; moving/reeling never teleports the body or resets the rope. Geometry, art, mass, damping, controls and pickup policy remain caller-owned.

`configure(tip, anchor, cable_length, polygons=[])` accepts a CableBody endpoint, world anchor, paid-out length and optional static wrapping geometry. `pivot_local` defaults to (0,-16), `load_mount_local` to (0,16), and `angle_limit` to ±35° from world upright. Callers update `anchor` and `cable_length`; their frame-to-frame differences supply hoist and reel velocities. Initialize out of solids and retain unit body scale. `reset(tip_position)` is an explicit restart operation that detaches, clears velocities/orientation and resets cable history; ordinary controls never call it.

The native force-integration callback computes pivot-point velocity and effective inverse mass including angular inertia. A unilateral impulse opposes extension, accounting for support/reel velocity, gravity prediction and bounded drift correction; slack cable cannot push. Forces act at the top pivot and naturally tilt the body. An anticipatory angular stop plus a hard cap bounds rotation without discarding ordinary swing. `tension` reports applied impulse / step, and `blocked` indicates route/length overconstraint. The controlled hoist remains an external motor rather than a recoiling dynamic body. Numerical cable drift and the configured tension cap are not a breakage model.

`attach(body, local_offset=ZERO)` couples a caller-owned dynamic load at its local grip. A damped spring applies equal/opposite forces at the load grip and the endpoint's bottom mount, so load motion and mass feed back into magnet translation/rotation. `detach()` wakes the load and preserves momentum. `attachment_changed(body)` reports changes; `enabled=false` detaches and prevents pickup while cable physics continues. Spring, damping and force cap remain tuning fields. Collision geometry and real-world magnetic eligibility are not inferred.

The existing rope particle renderer retains motion through taut movement/reeling and slack transitions; only explicit reset or obstacle topology remeshing rebuilds history. Godot interpolates body transforms between physics ticks; the cable draws matching interpolated particle positions each rendered frame. Explicit resets clear both body and cable visual history, and callers finish interpolation when pausing. Rendering never changes solver state. In the playable scene, background depth is -10, cable is 0 and magnet/hoist are 2, so the cable ends are covered by their art.

`labs/crane/lab.tscn` proves the subsystem with diagnostic shapes; `labs/salvage/` supplies provisional crane art and sorting rules. `tests/crane_test.gd` checks actual lift/release and cable reach. `tests/physical_suspension_test.gd` checks inertial lag, pivot-induced tilt, angular stops, slack without compression, load reaction and draw-order contracts. Existing rope tests cover motion continuity and static route topology. `tests/rope_interpolation_test.gd` samples render frames between 60 Hz ticks, checks both interpolated endpoints, solver non-mutation, pause and reset. Six physical deliveries remain covered by the combined round test.

Implementation follows Godot's [RigidBody2D force-integration callback](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_rigidbody2d.html) and [PhysicsDirectBodyState2D impulse/inertia APIs](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_physicsdirectbodystate2d.html). This is a massless cable model with a separate particle-based visual, not a chain of massive rigid links, dynamic obstacle wrapping, self-collision or a performance claim.
